The problem

One empty space.
Thousands of decisions.

The 8-puzzle is a 3 × 3 sliding-tile puzzle. A legal move slides one numbered tile into the blank space. The challenge is to transform an initial arrangement into a chosen goal arrangement—while a search algorithm decides which state to explore next.

Heuristics

h₁(n)

Misplaced tiles

Counts numbered tiles not in their goal positions. Simple and admissible, but it ignores how far each tile must travel.

h₂(n)

Manhattan distance

Sums each tile’s horizontal and vertical distance from its goal position. More informative and still admissible for the 8-puzzle.
